BO7 operator skins for level.botbb_cfg_bot_skins in mp_bot_bundles.gsc

Use an operator name for that operator in a random one of its skins, or a skin name for exactly that skin.
These are the game's internal names. [bundle: x] = a store-bundle skin; with a blueprint rolled, the bot may use
that bundle's blueprint (bundle_chance in the settings).
Every operator belongs to one side. Random skins always come from the bot's own side; a skin named in the settings
is worn as given, even an operator of the other side.

[plain] = a default or ranked-play skin; random picks skip these with skin_pool 1 or more (CDL team jerseys with 2
or more, everything but bundle skins with 3).
